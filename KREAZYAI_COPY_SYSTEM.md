## 1. Core Brand Voice Principles

| Pillar | How It Translates in Copy | What We Avoid |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Intelligent & Specific** | State exact capabilities: "Checks SKU stock, applies sizing charts, and captures verified orders." | "Leverage next-gen AI power for your business." |
| **Business-Focused** | Emphasize ROI, time savings, and conversion rates: "Recover 32% of abandoned chats in WhatsApp." | "Change the way you interact with customers." |
| **Calm & Confident** | Direct, factual assertions: "Autonomous 24/7 customer care with human handoff guardrails." | Excessive exclamation marks, hyperbolic adjectives ("revolutionary", "mind-blowing"). |
| **Human & Clear** | Plain English: "Add your return policies so KreazyAI can resolve refunds automatically." | Jargon overload ("Vectorized retrieval-augmented generative semantic graph"). |

## 2. Headline & Section Architecture Rules

Every major section across all public and workspace pages must adhere to the **3-Part Hierarchy**:

```
[ EYEBROW ]     -> 11–13px uppercase, bold, letter-spacing 0.05em, pill container
[ HEADLINE ]    -> Large editorial, tight line-height (1.14), text-wrap balance
[ SUBTITLE ]    -> 15–18px, 1.6 line-height, max-width 640px, specific business value
[ ACTIONS ]     -> Max 2 buttons: Primary pill CTA + Secondary pill CTA
```

## 3. Product & Workspace Copy Standards

### 3.1 AI Assistant Interface
- **Greeting:** *"KreazyAI Assistant is active. What would you like to review or automate today?"*
- **Suggested Actions:**
  - *"Summarize revenue across WhatsApp and Instagram today."*
  - *"Check low-stock products needing supplier reorder."*
  - *"Show recent conversations that required human escalation."*
- **Action Execution Confirmation:** *"Drafted autonomous message campaign for 142 VIP customers. Ready for review."*
- **Rule:** Never display internal chain-of-thought or raw JSON blobs to the end-user.

### 3.2 Form Validation & Error Messages
- **Wrong:** *"Error 500: Failed to fetch"*
- **Correct:** *"Unable to connect to the store catalog right now. Your data is secure. Please retry in a moment."*
- **Form Error:** *"Please provide a valid business email so we can verify your organization."*
- **Success State:** *"Product catalog synced successfully. 120 SKUs are now live in AI memory."*

### 3.3 Empty State Copy Pattern
Every empty state must answer:
1. **What is missing?**
2. **Why does it matter?**
3. **What should the user do next?**

**Example (Products Empty State):**
- **Title:** No products in catalog yet
- **Description:** Add your products or import from Shopify so KreazyAI can check stock and recommend items during customer chats.
- **CTA:** `Add Your First Product`

**Example (Conversations Empty State):**
- **Title:** All customer threads resolved
- **Description:** Your AI agents are actively monitoring WhatsApp and Instagram for new inbound inquiries.
- **CTA:** `Test Live Chat Ingest`
